I'm Mateusz, a solo software developer based in Poland. I build practical software solutions to solve real-world problems, and I use this space to document what I'm building, breaking, and learning along the way.

My day-to-day background is in web technologies—primarily **PHP, JavaScript, and TypeScript**. Over the past few years, I've designed and built custom applications for several businesses, focusing on clean, reliable code that gets the job done without unnecessary bloat. 

Lately, though, I've become a massive **Rust** fanboy, which has pulled me down a lot of interesting low-level rabbit holes.

## What I'm Building & Exploring

When I'm not writing web software, I spend my time experimenting with systems programming, infrastructure, and tools I wish existed:

* **Local-First & Interoperable Productivity:** I'm working toward building an offline-first knowledge and task system—taking inspiration from tools like Anytype for data ownership, paired with a cleaner UI and Morgen-style timeblocking and calendar sync. I'm a big believer in open standards like **WebDAV** so apps actually play nice with existing ecosystems, though I'm also exploring clean internal protocols like **Protobuf** for performance.
* **Graphics & Game Dev:** I've dabbled in Unity, Godot, and **Bevy**. Since 3D modeling and art aren't really my thing, most of those projects remain unfinished experiments—which actually pushed me toward wanting to learn computer graphics from the ground up. My goal is to dive into **shaders, wgpu, Vulkan, or OpenGL** to eventually write my own voxel-based game engine.
* **Self-Hosting & Networking:** I have a solid grasp of networking and run a custom home server that I built myself, where I self-host a variety of my own services and experiments.

## Beyond the Screen

* **Hardware & Tinkering:** I enjoy messing around with boards like **Arduino** and basic electronics whenever I want to write code that interacts with the physical world.
* **Audio Engineering:** Sound design and audio engineering are big hobbies of mine—to the point where I occasionally consider them as a parallel career path.
